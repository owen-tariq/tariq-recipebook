MODERN BREAKFAST
Avocado Toast
Prep Time5 Minutes
Cook Time5 Minutes
Ingredients
  • 1-2 slices of sturdy, thick-cut bread (sourdough, multigrain, or seeded)
  • 1 ripe Hass avocado (soft and yielding to gentle pressure)
  • 1 tsp freshly squeezed lemon or lime juice
  • Kosher salt or flaky sea salt
  • Freshly cracked black pepper
  • Optional: Extra-virgin olive oil, red pepper flakes, fried/poached egg, Everything Bagel Seasoning, cherry tomatoes, balsamic glaze, fresh basil, or smoked salmon and capers.
Steps to Cook
  1. Toast the bread: Toast the slices until dark golden brown so the exterior is crisp and sturdy enough to hold the avocado without getting soggy.
  2. Mash the avocado: Scoop the avocado flesh into a shallow bowl. Add the lemon/lime juice, salt, and pepper. Mash with a fork until it reaches your desired consistency (leaving a few chunks provides great texture).
  3. Assemble: Divide the mixture and spread it edge-to-edge over your warm toast. Season with a final pinch of flaky salt.
  4. Add Upgrades (Optional): Top with a fried egg, a drizzle of olive oil, Everything Bagel Seasoning, or any of your favorite gourmet toppings. Serve immediately.
