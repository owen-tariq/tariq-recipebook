QUICK & SIMPLE BENGALI CUISINE
Faakibaji Fish Curry (Rui)
Prep Time10 Minutes
Cook Time20 Minutes
Ingredients
  • 4 pieces of Rui (Rohu) fish
  • For Marination: Salt, Turmeric powder, and Red Chilli powder
  • 3-4 tbsp Mustard oil or Vegetable oil
  • 2 medium Onions (sliced)
  • Salt (to taste)
  • 1 tsp Red Chilli powder
  • 1 tsp Coriander powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin powder
  • 1 medium Tomato (sliced)
  • 3-4 Green Chillies (sliced)
  • A handful of fresh Coriander leaves (chopped)
Steps to Cook
  1. Marinate the Fish: Rub the Rui fish pieces with a pinch of salt, turmeric powder, and red chilli powder.
  2. Fry the Fish: Heat oil in a pan. Fry the marinated fish pieces until they are golden brown on both sides. Remove from the pan and set aside.
  3. Sauté the Onions: In the same oil, add the sliced onions and fry until they get soft and light brown.
  4. Add Spices: Once the onions are soft, add salt and red chilli powder. Stir well, then add the coriander powder and cumin powder. Fry the spices for a few moments until fragrant.
  5. Add Tomatoes & Chillies: Add the sliced tomatoes and green chillies to the pan. Sauté until the tomatoes soften slightly and blend with the spices.
  6. Simmer with Fish: Add the fried fish pieces back into the pan. Mix gently so the fish is coated with the spices (you can add a tiny splash of water if the gravy is too dry). Let it simmer for a couple of minutes.
  7. Finish & Garnish: Finish off by sprinkling generously with chopped fresh coriander leaves. Serve hot with plain rice!
