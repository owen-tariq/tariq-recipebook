RUSTIC VILLAGE STYLE
Classic Gramer Murgi (Village Style Chicken Curry)
Prep Time10 Minutes
Cook Time1 Hour
Ingredients (Serves 4)
  • 1 kg Bone-in Chicken Thighs (or 500g Boneless)
  • 4 Onions (about 500g, sliced)
  • 1 Whole Garlic Bulb
  • 8-10 cloves of Garlic (peeled)
  • 2 tbsp Ginger Garlic paste
  • 2 Green Chillies (slit)
  • 3 dried Red Chillies
  • 3 Bay leaves
  • 3 Green Cardamom pods
  • 2 Cinnamon sticks
  • 8-10 Black Peppercorns
  • 150ml Mustard Oil (or to your liking)
  • 1/2 tsp Red Chilli powder
  • 2 tsp Coriander powder
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1½ tsp Salt
  • 1 tsp Cumin seeds
  • 1½ tsp Garam Masala
Nutrition per serving:
Calories: 311 | Protein: 35g | Carbs: 16g | Fats: 12g
Steps to Cook
  1. Combine Ingredients: In a large pot, add the chicken along with all the spices, onions, garlic, chillies, and mustard oil.
  2. Mix Well: Mix everything together very well so the chicken is fully coated with the oil and spices.
  3. Start Cooking: Put the pot on the stove. Cover it with a lid and drop the heat to low.
  4. Stir Often: After 10 to 15 minutes, open the lid and stir well. Continue to stir often so the chicken does not stick to the bottom of the pot.
  5. Simmer Until Ready: Let it cook slowly on low heat. (Note: You can add 1 cup of water after 40 minutes if you feel like adding it). After 1 hour, your delicious village-style chicken will be ready to serve!
