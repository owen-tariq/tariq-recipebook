TRADITIONAL VEGAN BANGLA
Classic Bangladeshi Lentil Soup (Daal)
Prep Time5 Minutes
Cook Time25 Minutes
Ingredients
  • 100g Yellow Lentil (Muug Daal)
  • 100g Orange Lentil (Mashur Daal)
  • (Or 200g of any kind of lentil)
  • 50ml Vegetable Oil
  • 1 large Onion (sliced)
  • 1 inch Ginger (minced)
  • 2-3 cloves of Garlic (sliced)
  • 2 tsp Salt
  • 2-3 dried Red Chilies
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 Bay leaf
  • 1 tbs Whole Cumin seeds
Steps to Cook
  1. Wash & Soak: Wash lentils with lukewarm water 7-8 times until the water comes out clean. Soak them in water for 30 minutes.
  2. Boil Lentils: Boil the rinsed lentils in a medium pot with double the amount of water, salt, turmeric, and 3-4 cloves of garlic. Cook on medium heat for 20 minutes until they are half-broken (use a hand blender if needed). Do not drain the water.
  3. Prepare Spices (Phoron): Heat the vegetable oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Add the ginger, bay leaf, and dried chilies first. After a few seconds, add the whole cumin and remaining garlic. Cook until bubbling.
  4. Sauté Onions: Add the sliced onions to the pan and sauté on low heat until they are golden brown.
  5. Mix: Scoop a ladle full of the boiled lentils and pour it into the fried onion mixture in the pan. Simmer for 10 seconds to catch all the flavor.
  6. Combine: Pour the lentil and spice mixture from the pan back into the main pot with the rest of the boiled lentils.
  7. Simmer & Serve: Simmer the lentils for 5 to 6 minutes on low heat. Add extra water if you want a thinner soup. Check the salt and adjust if necessary. Serve hot with rice, roti, or naan!
