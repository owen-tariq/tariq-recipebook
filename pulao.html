PERFECT PAIRING
Plain Pulao (Sada Polao)
Prep Time10 Minutes
Cook Time25 Minutes
Ingredients
  • Aromatic Rice: 2 cups (Chinigura or Kalijira preferred)
  • Oil: 3 tbsp
  • Ghee: 1 tbsp
  • Onions: 2 medium, thinly sliced
  • Ginger Paste: 1 tsp
  • Garlic Paste: 1 tsp
  • Whole Spices: 2 cardamoms, 2 cinnamon sticks, 3 cloves, 2 bay leaves
  • Salt: 1 tsp (or to taste)
  • Liquid: 3 cups hot water + 1 cup liquid milk or Coconut Milk
  • Fried Onion
Steps to Cook
  1. Prepare the Rice: Wash thoroughly and drain at least 30 minutes.
  2. Sauté Whole Spices: Heat oil and ghee. Add cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, bay leaves.
  3. Soften Onions: Fry until soft and translucent. Do not brown.
  4. Fry the Rice: Add drained rice and sauté. Stir constantly to keep grains separate.
  5. Add Aromatics: Add ginger paste, garlic paste, and salt. Continue sautéing until rice feels light.
  6. Add Liquids: Pour 3 cups hot water and 1 cup milk.
  7. Simmer: Bring to boil. When water level drops, reduce heat.
  8. Steam (Dum): Cover tightly and steam on low for 10 minutes.
  9. Final Rest: Turn off heat. Fluff gently, add Fried Onion and rest 5 minutes before serving.
