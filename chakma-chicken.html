CHAKMA TRADITIONAL CUISINE
Chakma Style Chicken Curry
Prep Time20 Minutes
Cook Time45 Minutes
Ingredients (Serves 4)
  • 1 kg Bone-in Chicken (medium to small pieces)
  • 3–4 Potatoes (peeled and halved)
  • 2–3 large Onions (finely chopped)
  • 2 tbsp Ginger Paste
  • 1.5 tbsp Garlic Paste
  • 2–3 Green Chillies (made into paste)
  • 1 tsp Salt (plus more to taste)
  • 1 tsp Chicken Masala
  • 1 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Cumin Powder
  • 1 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1 tsp Coriander (whole or ground)
  • 80 ml Cooking Oil
  • 1.5–2 cups Water
  • Fresh Coriander Leaves (chopped, for garnish)
Nutrition per serving:
Calories: ~340 | Protein: 38g | Carbs: 18g | Fats: 13g
Steps to Cook
  1. Clean the Chicken: Wash the chicken thoroughly under cold running water. Make sure the pieces are medium to small in size. Drain all the water from the chicken completely.
  2. Marinate: Add salt, 1 tsp chicken masala, and turmeric to the chicken. Mix well so every piece is evenly coated. Set aside.
  3. Prepare the Aromatics: Finely chop the onions. Make a smooth paste from the ginger and garlic. Separately, make a paste from the green chillies.
  4. Fry the Onions: Add oil to a pan or pot over medium heat. Add the chopped onions and fry until they turn soft — do not let them brown.
  5. Add the Pastes: Add the ginger paste, garlic paste, and green chilli paste. Fry for a couple of minutes until fragrant.
  6. Add the Spices: Reduce the heat to low. Add the coriander, red chilli powder, chicken masala, cumin powder, and coriander powder. Mix everything together well. Add a small splash of water to prevent the spices from burning.
  7. Add the Chicken: Increase the heat to medium. Once the oil starts to separate from the masala, add the marinated chicken. Mix thoroughly so the chicken is fully coated in the masala. Cover the pot with a lid.
  8. Cook the Chicken: Stir every 2–3 minutes to prevent sticking. The chicken will begin to release its own water — let it cook in that liquid.
  9. Add the Potatoes: Once the chicken has released its water, add the potatoes and stir them in.
  10. Final Simmer: Once the released water has evaporated, add 1.5 to 2 cups of fresh water. Taste and adjust salt as needed. Cook on low to medium heat for a further 20 minutes, or until the chicken and potatoes are fully tender.
  11. Garnish and Serve: Scatter freshly chopped coriander leaves on top and serve hot.
