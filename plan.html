Use the freshest items first

Cook or freeze raw chicken, fish and shrimp promptly. Keep uncooked meat covered and separately on the lowest fridge shelf.

Keep a small repeatable pantry

Rice, soy sauce without alcohol, cumin, paprika, garlic and cooking oil can work across many weeks. Don’t buy every specialty sauce at once.

Only purchase the missing extras

Check what's already in your kitchen, choose a single option where a recipe says “or”, and buy shared vegetables in reusable quantities.
